About the Facility

Advanced environmental research infrastructure dedicated to microplastic detection, characterization, monitoring, and environmental impact assessment.

Key Analytical Capabilities

  • Microplastic extraction from water & sediment
  • Density separation techniques
  • Microscopic size classification
  • Shape analysis (fiber, fragment, bead, film, pellet)
  • Color categorization
  • Polymer identification using FTIR

Research Focus Areas

  • Coastal and estuarine microplastic pollution
  • Urban wastewater contamination
  • Polymer distribution studies
  • Microplastic–heavy metal interaction
