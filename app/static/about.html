About Us

Overview

The Marine Litter and Microplastics (MLMP) Research Laboratory, established in 2022 at SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST), Kattankulathur (KTR Campus) under the Department of Civil Engineering, is located in the Main Campus (MB–16). The laboratory operates with funding support from the National Centre for Coastal Research (NCCR), Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES), Government of India (GoI).

The MLMP Lab is dedicated to comprehensive research on marine litter and microplastics, focusing on both qualitative and quantitative assessment in water, sediment, and biota samples. The facility is equipped with advanced analytical infrastructure, including density separation systems, wet peroxide oxidation units, sieving and filtration assemblies, and stereo zoom and digital microscopy for microplastic identification.

The laboratory follows standardized sampling protocols and strict contamination control measures to ensure data accuracy and reliability. Research activities emphasize morphological identification, size and shape classification, abundance estimation, and environmental risk assessment. Through its systematic monitoring and analytical approach, the MLMP Lab contributes to scientific research, environmental sustainability, and policy-supportive studies on coastal and marine ecosystems.

Key Analytical Capabilities

  • plastic and Microplastic extraction from water and sediment
  • Density separation techniques
  • Microscopic size classification
  • Shape analysis (fiber, fragment, bead, film, pellet)
  • Color categorization
  • Polymer identification using FTIR

Research Focus Areas

  • Coastal microplastic pollution
  • Estuarine pollution studies
  • Urban wastewater contamination
  • Polymer distribution analysis
  • Microplastic–heavy metal interaction
